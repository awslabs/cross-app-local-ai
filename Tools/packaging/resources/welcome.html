Welcome to FastLang

FastLang is a macOS utility for fast, hotkey-driven text generation and refinement. It can run fully offline using a local Gemma model, or connect to a remote LLM provider.

On the Customize screen you'll pick which Gemma model to use:

  • E2B (~3 GB) — balanced speed and quality. Recommended for most users.
  • E4B (~4.8 GB) — higher quality at a larger size.

The chosen model downloads automatically the first time you launch the app, with a progress bar in Settings. You can change your pick later without reinstalling.

Note: this installer is unsigned. You may need to right-click the package and choose Open the first time, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
